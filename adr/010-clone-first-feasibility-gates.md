# ADR-010 — Clone-First Feasibility Gates

**Status:** Accepted
**Architecture:** 1.0

## Context

A weak CAD/BIM or Project foundation would undermine the entire product thesis.

## Decision

CAD/BIM and Project compatibility research is the first major implementation wave. Their benchmark gates precede deep construction intelligence implementation.

## Consequences

Early work is research/benchmark-heavy, but existential product risks are exposed before expensive downstream development.
